Last year, Tom decided to improve his English. At first, the task seemed impossible, and he almost wanted to abandon the plan. But his teacher told him that real ability grows from daily effort. So Tom made a simple schedule: twenty new words every day, and he would not neglect any of them. Slowly, he began to recognize patterns and understand the logic behind each word. When he met a difficult problem, he did not refuse to face it. Instead, he tried to find a better method. After six months, he could read short articles without fear. His steady progress proved that continued work can achieve great results.
